What a Tawqee signature is, and what it isn't.

Know what you're getting before you decide which documents to route through it.

Tawqee doesn't use certificate-based signatures

Tawqee places a visible signature on the PDF and records who signed, when, and what they agreed to. It doesn't apply a cryptographic certificate (PKI) signature, so PDF readers won't show the file as digitally signed or certified, and Tawqee makes no legal-validity claim.

Use it for internal approvals and everyday documents such as letters, requisitions and sign-offs. For contracts that need legal assurance, or signers outside your organisation, use a certificate-based e-signature service.

Recorded with every action

  • The signer's SharePoint identity, the stage and the document revision
  • The exact consent statement they accepted
  • SHA-256 fingerprints of the PDF before and after signing
  • Every signed version, kept alongside the original and never overwritten
  • The reason given for any change request or decline

Checked before anything changes

  • Only the current stage's person can act, and only on the current revision
  • The PDF service refuses a document whose fingerprint doesn't match the recorded one
  • Encrypted PDFs, and PDFs that already carry a certificate signature, are refused
  • The PDF service processes each file and keeps nothing afterwards
  • No public links or thumbnails are created for signature images

Limits to know

  • People with edit rights on the SharePoint site can change records outside Tawqee. App checks prevent mistakes, not determined misuse.
  • SharePoint version history helps recovery. It isn't tamper-proof evidence.
  • Action times come from the user's browser, not a trusted timestamp service.

Does Tawqee run inside SharePoint?

Yes. Tawqee is a SharePoint Framework web part added to a page on your organisation's SharePoint site. This website introduces the product; your team uses the app in SharePoint.

Where are documents stored?

Requests, tasks and files are stored in SharePoint lists and a document library in your site. To place a signature, the PDF is sent to a dedicated PDF service that stamps it and returns it. The result is saved back to SharePoint, and the service keeps nothing.

Is a Tawqee signature legally binding?

Tawqee makes no legal-validity claim. It isn't certificate-based signing: it applies a visible signature with an activity record, suited to internal approvals and everyday documents. See what's recorded, and the limits.

Can people outside our organisation sign?

No. Participants are internal users of your organisation. Guest and external signing aren't included.

Can we fix the approval route for certain documents?

Yes. Site owners create document types from roles such as Finance Head or CFO. A requester picks the type, and the route is filled in and locked. Requesters can still choose General to build their own route.

Does every request need a signature?

No. A route can be approvals only, and it completes without changing the PDF.

Does it send email notifications?

Not in this version. Each person sees what's waiting for them under My tasks in the app.
